Introduction

I used to have a blog years ago, but abandoned the idea after realising all I was posting were just ramblings.

Now, I want to try again, but this time with a more focused approach. I want to write about my experiences as I set off on a new journey in to web development. Despite having a pretty intermediate understanding of building websites - I want to document my journey as I learn more about the field, and hopefully share some useful insights along the way.

So, if you are interested in web development, or just want to follow along with my journey, then please feel free to check back here regularly for new posts. I will be posting about my experiences, what I have learned, and any useful resources I come across.
