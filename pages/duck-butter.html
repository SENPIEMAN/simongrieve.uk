Timeline

During the Pandemic, I started a podcast with a friend of mine. We talked about Video Games, and the industry in general. We had a lot of fun doing it, and it was a great way to keep in touch with people during a time when we were all stuck at home.

We recorded the podcast over Discord, and edited it ourselves. We released it on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and other platforms.

We eventually stopped recording the podcast, but it was a great experience and I learned a lot about audio production and editing.
