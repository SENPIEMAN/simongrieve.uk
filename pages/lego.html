My "mid-life crisis"

{% include breadcrumbs.html %}

Towards the end of 2025 I purchased my first Lego kit. Lego wasn't a luxury my parents could afford as a child, and as such wasn't something I showed an interest in.

It wasn't until a trip to the Lego Store in Battersea at Christmas last year that I thought I'd buy one - the McLaren Speed Champions Car. That, as they say, was how it all started.
