# Hello World..? 
I have no idea how to even start this...

Welcome to my blog, I guess?

I decided to build this site on Jekyll mainly because I use Markdown a lot & am very familiar with it. When I was looking at options I considered what I already knew - and what services/platforms I already knew. I wanted something that was easy to use, and easy to host. I also wanted something that was easy to maintain, and easy to update. I also wanted something that was simple, and didn't require a lot of overhead. Jekyll ticks all of those boxes.

I'm very familiar with things like **Astro** and **Docusaurus** from the work I do with Casterlabs. I have used both when writing our documentation. But I wanted something... minimalist, something that kept with the the aesthetic of the rest of the site. Besides, I host this site on GitHub (which works perfectly) I kind of already know Jekyll pretty well. 

It's 2026 - nobody uses WordPress anymore.

So here we are!

## Why a Blog?
I pretty much explained it in the opening blurb on the front page. I've always had an interest in web development. This is the `*counts*` seventh website I've had. They were all built without a real purpose *(excluding the ones I made when I was a livestreamer)* and - as I said - the ones that *were* blogs were just full of ramblings!

I'm about to start doing some online courses, focussing not just on web development, but also Computer Science. As an aid to my learning, I think a blog sounds like a good idea!

This is not going to be a livejournal full of emo I hate life 🪒🩸 type shit. Not any more. There certainly won't be any *Doctor Who (2005)* episode reviews either. 

I'm in my fourties now ffs.
