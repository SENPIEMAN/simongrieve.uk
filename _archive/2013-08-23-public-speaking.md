## Public Speaking

I’ve never given a speech before. I’ve been trying to write this thing for the last three days now.

I can barely maintain a blog — and yet I’m expected to write a speech? That I have to read out? To people. Lots of people.

Panic Attacks are a lot easier to handle if you write down exactly what is stressing you out, share it and then, have the Panic Attack anyway.

---

So, Mum and Stepdad are getting married in just over two weeks. And your’s truly has to give her away; as I’m the eldest child. Apparently, one of my “duties” is to give a speech at the end of the meal! I’ve been told I can’t proxy it either. Drat.

I’ve got quite a lot of it written already. But, I’m facing the whole “It took me an hour to write, I thought it might take an hour to read” problem.

I think I might just go hyperventilate in to a paper bag some more.
