It’s a shame that this website has not reached its full potential.
When I first launched it I had all these ideas for what I was going to do with it, and how I was going to use it. The best laid schemes etc.

So I’ve decided to do something about it – now that I have a bit of spare time on my hands.

Yesterday I started working on completely rebuilding using Dreamweaver. It’s still only one page, at the moment — but I have a pretty good idea of what I want to achieve with it. I really need to start writing down ideas when I have them, as well. The problem is I always have them when I’m in bed or something, and by the time it comes to do any work on the pages, they’ve gone.

I’m going to build a full website, no more of this minimalist ‘splash pages’ design. I’m going to make it fully functional.
Kind of like I did when I used iWeb to make the original simbaclauson.

I am going to be taking my time with the project, however. I always rush out my websites, and it shows; I think.

That’s the dream. Let’s make it a reality.

God, that’s so cliché.
